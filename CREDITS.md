# Credits

Sahuarita Smash! is Andrew Saulls's own game: the fighters, stages, moves, pictures (drawn in code), sounds (made in
code) and the built-in music are original. The recorded songs in `Music/Brawl` (menu, the five stages, victory) are
Andrew's own, made in Suno on a Pro plan; never add a song made on a free Suno plan or by anyone else. It was first made for App Hub's Classic Games (2026-10-29).

Libraries and fonts bundled with the app are listed in `saucestore.json` (`credits`); keep that list complete for every
third-party file, library, font or recording.

Not affiliated with Nintendo or any other game's makers. The historical figures (Theodore Roosevelt, Benjamin
Franklin) are respectful caricatures of people who died long ago; Paul Bunyan is a folk legend.
