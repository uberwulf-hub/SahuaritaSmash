# Changelog

Everything that changes for the people who play, in plain words. Newest first. The lines under a version are its
release notes (### Added / ### Fixed become New / Fixed in SauceStore).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Bluetooth game controllers work on Android phones and tablets: pair an Xbox, PlayStation or other gamepad with
  the phone and play with it, up to four at once (the first one picked up is controller 1). The game says
  "Controller 1 connected", and the touch controls keep working beside the first controller.

## [0.3.1] - 2026-11-16

### Fixed
- The phone's volume buttons turn the game's music and sounds up and down again (the game's screen was taking them).
- Your own songs no longer start with a second of the old built-in tune: the game waits for the song to be ready (the
  first screen may be quiet for a moment while the app starts), and only plays the built-in tune if a song takes very
  long or can't be read.
- The pause menu has big buttons you can tap on a phone: Keep playing, Restart, Main menu, music and sounds (before,
  it listed keyboard keys, and its rows were too small to tap). Playing online, the menu has Back to the game and
  Leave this game.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added
- A new fighter from Arizona: Gila Gus, a beaded orange-and-black Gila monster. Slow and heavy, with a tongue that
  flicks, a bite that won't let go, a rock hop home and a tail that sweeps the ground.
- A new stage: the Pecan Orchard, Sahuarita's own. Fight on the branches, the harvest bins and the picker's basket
  going up and down; when the tree shaker rumbles, get out from under the shadows before the pecans land. It has its
  own tune for now.
- Online games need everyone on this version (the cast and the stages changed).

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-15

### Added
- Play online, like Race Track Mayhem: host a game on your home network (the others find it under Join) or over
  the internet (a 4-letter code for family in other houses), pick fighters in the lobby, add computer players, and
  fight on separate computers, phones and tablets. Everyone's match stays exactly the same.

### Fixed
- A slow build-up of memory over many matches (each stage's background is now let go when the next one starts).

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-14

### Changed
- Android's touch controls: no Jump button (push the stick up to jump), and Shield is now big, right beside
  Attack (hold Shield and push the stick to roll or dodge).
- The game fills the whole screen: no title bar on Windows (F11 or Alt+Enter for a window), and no status or
  navigation bar on Android (swipe from the edge to see them).

### Fixed
- No sound on Android: the music, the sounds and the songs play now, and the phone's volume buttons turn the game
  up and down.
- The music changes smoothly: all of Andrew's songs are ready a few seconds after the game starts, so a screen or
  stage no longer starts with the old chiptune and then switches to its song.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added
- Sahuarita Smash! is its own app now, on Windows and Android (it was a game in App Hub's Classic Games).
- Ten fighters, each with 13 attacks of its own and four specials, on five stages with surprises that always warn
  first (monsoon wind, a javelina stampede, mine carts, meteors, a lab laser).
- Up to four players, people or the computer at four levels, and the ladder: six matches that end against a giant.
- Change your keys on Windows; Xbox-style controllers work there too.
- Touch controls on Android: a stick under your left thumb and big buttons for your right.
- The music and sounds, with Andrew's own songs for the menu, every stage and the victory.
- On Windows, your Classic Games ladder scores and keys come across the first time it starts.
