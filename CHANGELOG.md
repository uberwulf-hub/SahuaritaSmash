# Changelog

Everything that changes for the people who play, in plain words. Newest first. The lines under a version are its
release notes (### Added / ### Fixed become New / Fixed in SauceStore).

## [Unreleased]

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-21

### Added
- On the title screen, Left and Right now jump between the two columns of buttons (Up and Down work as before).

### Changed
- A new title screen: a bolder logo, big buttons in two columns, and the whole cast standing in two rows beside them.
- Teddy has a new look: a green safari jacket, a bigger friendlier face with round glasses, a big moustache and grin,
  and his big stick rests on his shoulder until he swings it. His old tan look is now his unlockable "Khaki" colour.
- The Back button is bigger, so it's easy to tap on a phone.

## [0.4.3] - 2026-11-20

### Changed
- Monsoon Wash's flash flood looks like real rushing water now: teal-grey water with a rolling, foamy front, splashes
  and droplets, and streaks and swirls that race along with it, instead of a brown slope that looked like dirt. The
  trickle before it arrives and the water pouring off the edge match. It moves and bonks exactly as before.

## [0.4.2] - 2026-11-20

### Changed
- Monsoon Wash looks like the Sonoran Desert now: blue palo verde with green trunks and airy crowns, twisting
  velvet mesquite, desert shrubs and a few saguaros far off, instead of trees that looked like they came from Africa.
- The "Do not enter when flooded" sign is a yellow diamond on a post with a depth gauge, standing by the log at the
  wash's entrance, where the footbridge can't hide it any more.

## [0.4.1] - 2026-11-20

### Changed
- Easy and Normal computer players are much gentler: they walk up instead of running, take fewer chances, rest
  between attacks, rarely go for big finishing hits, and Easy sometimes just stands and watches. Easy now does about a
  third of the damage it used to, and the Easy ladder stays Easy the whole way up (Normal's climbs only a little).
  Hard and Champion are just as before.
- Online games need everyone on this version (the computer players changed).

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- Bluetooth game controllers work on Android phones and tablets: pair an Xbox, PlayStation or other gamepad with
  the phone and play with it, up to four at once (the first one picked up is controller 1). The game says
  "Controller 1 connected", and the touch controls keep working beside the first controller.
- On a phone, the pause menu now shows how to play by touch, in big letters: a picture of the buttons and your
  fighter's moves and specials the touch way (instead of the keyboard table).
- Touch controls settings (on the title screen while playing by touch): bigger or smaller buttons, a left-handed
  layout (the stick on the right, the buttons on the left) and vibration on or off. Each phone remembers its own.
- The phone gives a short buzz when your fighter lands a hit or gets hit (a longer one for a big hit or a bonk off
  the stage). It can be turned off under Touch controls.
- The first time you fight on a phone, a short guide shows how the touch controls work (four cards you can skip). It
  can be shown again from Touch controls.
- Practice (on the title screen): pick your fighter and a stage and try your moves on a dummy. Nobody loses: fall
  off and you're straight back, with no timer and no scores. The dummy stands still, or walks, jumps, shields or
  fights back (gently) if you ask; the menu also puts everyone's damage back to 0%, turns stage surprises on or off,
  and shows all your moves with their names. Your specials and big attacks are listed on the screen while you play.
- New colours to unlock: climb the ladder with a fighter to unlock its second colour, and climb it on Hard for a
  third. Choose them with the colour buttons under your fighter (or C on the keyboard). Two players with the same
  fighter get different colours when they can. Online games use the usual colours.
- Playing online, after the results the host can tap Rematch (the same fighters on the same stage, straight into the
  countdown for everyone) or Back to the lobby; the others see what the host is choosing and can leave.
- A new fighter from the tall tales: Pecos Bill, the cowboy raised by coyotes. Long-legged and quick, with a lasso
  that ropes you from across the stage (or pulls you in and throws you), a little twister he rides up into the sky,
  and a coyote howl that pushes everyone near him away.
- A new stage: Monsoon Wash, a dry sandy wash in Sahuarita with a storm flashing over the mountains. Fight on a fallen
  log, the old footbridge and the culvert. When the thunder rolls and the sign says "Flash flood! Get up high", jump
  or climb: a wave of muddy water runs along the ground and sweeps along anyone standing in it (a light bump, never
  over the edge). It has its own tune for now.
- Online games need everyone on this version (the cast, the stages and the rematch changed).

## [0.3.1] - 2026-11-16

### Fixed
- The phone's volume buttons turn the game's music and sounds up and down again (the game's screen was taking them).
- Your own songs no longer start with a second of the old built-in tune: the game waits for the song to be ready (the
  first screen may be quiet for a moment while the app starts), and only plays the built-in tune if a song takes very
  long or can't be read.
- The pause menu has big buttons you can tap on a phone: Keep playing, Restart, Main menu, music and sounds (before,
  it listed keyboard keys, and its rows were too small to tap). Playing online, the menu has Back to the game and
  Leave this game.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added
- A new fighter from Arizona: Gila Gus, a beaded orange-and-black Gila monster. Slow and heavy, with a tongue that
  flicks, a bite that won't let go, a rock hop home and a tail that sweeps the ground.
- A new stage: the Pecan Orchard, Sahuarita's own. Fight on the branches, the harvest bins and the picker's basket
  going up and down; when the tree shaker rumbles, get out from under the shadows before the pecans land. It has its
  own tune for now.
- Online games need everyone on this version (the cast and the stages changed).

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-15

### Added
- Play online, like Race Track Mayhem: host a game on your home network (the others find it under Join) or over
  the internet (a 4-letter code for family in other houses), pick fighters in the lobby, add computer players, and
  fight on separate computers, phones and tablets. Everyone's match stays exactly the same.

### Fixed
- A slow build-up of memory over many matches (each stage's background is now let go when the next one starts).

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-14

### Changed
- Android's touch controls: no Jump button (push the stick up to jump), and Shield is now big, right beside
  Attack (hold Shield and push the stick to roll or dodge).
- The game fills the whole screen: no title bar on Windows (F11 or Alt+Enter for a window), and no status or
  navigation bar on Android (swipe from the edge to see them).

### Fixed
- No sound on Android: the music, the sounds and the songs play now, and the phone's volume buttons turn the game
  up and down.
- The music changes smoothly: all of Andrew's songs are ready a few seconds after the game starts, so a screen or
  stage no longer starts with the old chiptune and then switches to its song.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added
- Sahuarita Smash! is its own app now, on Windows and Android (it was a game in App Hub's Classic Games).
- Ten fighters, each with 13 attacks of its own and four specials, on five stages with surprises that always warn
  first (monsoon wind, a javelina stampede, mine carts, meteors, a lab laser).
- Up to four players, people or the computer at four levels, and the ladder: six matches that end against a giant.
- Change your keys on Windows; Xbox-style controllers work there too.
- Touch controls on Android: a stick under your left thumb and big buttons for your right.
- The music and sounds, with Andrew's own songs for the menu, every stage and the victory.
- On Windows, your Classic Games ladder scores and keys come across the first time it starts.
